The objective of this lab is to analyze a suspicious email using several tools to confirm whether it is malicious or not. To do this, we will use resources from the BlueDefenders platform, specifically the PhishStrike lab. We will analyze the email headers and threat intelligence to identify indicators of phishing, malware persistence, and C2 channels, extracting actionable indicators of competence (IOCs) and answering related questions.
